### Part 3: 实用短文阅读理解 (Short Reading Comprehension)
*阅读下方短文，回答后面两道选择题。*

> Many people think learning a language as an adult is nearly impossible, but scientific research shows otherwise. While children are naturally good at picking up native-like accents, adults have stronger analytical skills and can understand complex grammatical rules much faster. The most critical factor is not your age, but how often you use the language in real-life contexts. Consistency matters far more than intensity.

9. According to the text, what is the main advantage adults have in language learning?
   - A. They have better accents than children.
   - B. They have stronger analytical skills to grasp grammar quickly.
   - C. They have much more free time to practice.
   - D. They memorize new words without any effort.

   - A. Learning before adulthood.
   - B. Studying intensely for 10 hours once a month.
